# Design Brief

Written by the manager BEFORE frontend work begins. Its job is to give frontend a
product-specific design *problem*, not a style instruction.

Frontend will refuse to proceed on a brief that has no reference points. "Modern",
"clean", and "professional" are not answers to anything below.

---

## Target audience

Who uses this, in what physical and emotional situation? Standing on a sidewalk
in the sun on a cracked phone screen is a different design problem from a desk at
9am.

## Primary interface job

The one thing the interface must make effortless. Not a feature list.

## Signature element

One idea that could not appear in a different product. Not a logo. A structural,
typographic, or interaction idea that comes out of what this product actually does.

If you cannot name one, you have not finished thinking about the product.
